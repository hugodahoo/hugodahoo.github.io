## About Section

**Instructions:** Copy-paste the text below into your LinkedIn About section. The first 3 lines (before "see more") are the hook -- they must stand alone and compel a click.

---

I build real-time systems that turn physical space into responsive, perceptual environments. As Lead Creative Technologist at Atelier Lozano-Hemmer, I've led the technical development of installations across contemporary art museums, mega-galleries, heritage buildings, underground cisterns, and open desert -- from Pace Gallery and the Museo de Arte Moderno in Mexico City to a 19th-century frontón in Madrid and a 2km night trail through Abu Dhabi's dunes -- working alongside one of the most exhibited media artists in the world.

Before that, I spent four years at Moment Factory building the visual systems behind the Red Hot Chili Peppers, Arcade Fire, and Billie Eilish world tours, plus interactive exhibitions in Tokyo, Hong Kong, New York, and Beijing. Two of those projects won Frame Awards -- Set Design of the Year and Best Use of Digital Technology.

Day-to-day, I create visually compelling graphics using TouchDesigner and other tools, wrangle thermal cameras, LiDAR, and cosmic ray detectors, design spatial audio for hundreds of channels, and make it all hold up in architecturally singular spaces. I lead projects end-to-end -- from early R&D through on-site commissioning -- and I've shipped 50+ productions across five continents.

I enjoy working at the edge of what’s technically possible... and just beyond what’s reasonable.
