# LinkedIn Banner Image Brief

## Technical Specs

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Dimensions | **1584 x 396 px** (4:1 ratio) |
| Format | JPG or PNG |
| Max file size | 8 MB |
| Safe zone | Profile photo overlaps **bottom-left corner** on desktop. Keep focal content **center to right**. |
| Mobile crop | Mobile crops tighter vertically -- keep important elements in the **center 60%** of height |

## Recommended Approach

Use a **cinematic still frame from one of your installations** -- no text overlay needed. Your work is visually striking enough to speak for itself. Optionally, add a subtle `hugodaoust.com` watermark in the bottom-right corner.

## Source Image Candidates (ranked)

### 1. Kristallstimmen (Swarovski Crystal Worlds, 2024)
- **Why:** 3,000 crystal-clad speakers with illumination -- visually stunning, the scale is immediately impressive. The dark background with glowing points of light works perfectly in the wide 4:1 ratio.
- **Source:** Check Lozano-Hemmer's media archive or https://www.lozano-hemmer.com/kristallstimmen.php
- **Crop note:** A wide shot showing the full canopy of speakers works best. Avoid tight crops that lose the sense of scale.

### 2. Collider (Abu Dhabi / Parrish Art Museum)
- **Why:** Curtains of light from cosmic rays at night -- spectacular, immediately eye-catching. The horizontal lines of light naturally suit the wide banner format.
- **Source:** https://www.lozano-hemmer.com/collider.php or Vimeo stills
- **Crop note:** The Abu Dhabi version (searchlights) is more dramatic. A wide shot from a distance showing the full curtain works perfectly.

### 3. Shadow Tuner (Abu Dhabi)
- **Why:** The glowing Earth with human silhouettes -- immediately compelling and mysterious. Communicates "interactive" at a glance.
- **Source:** https://www.lozano-hemmer.com/shadow_tuner.php

### 4. Climate Parliament (Rice University, Houston)
- **Why:** 481 pendant speaker-lights forming a canopy -- the repetition and warm glow create a beautiful wide composition.
- **Source:** https://www.lozano-hemmer.com/climate_parliament.php

### 5. Translation Island (Abu Dhabi, night shots)
- **Why:** Multiple installations in a desert landscape at night. If there's a wide establishing shot, it could be stunning.

## How to Create the Banner

### Option A: Quick (10 min)
1. Find a high-resolution photo of the chosen installation (ask the studio archive or download from lozano-hemmer.com press kit if available)
2. Open in any image editor (Photoshop, Figma, Canva, even Preview on Mac)
3. Crop to 1584 x 396 px, keeping focal point center-right
4. Export as JPG at 80%+ quality
5. Upload to LinkedIn

### Option B: Polished (30 min)
1. Same as above, but:
2. Add a very subtle dark gradient on the left edge (where your profile photo will overlap) to ensure contrast
3. Slightly boost contrast/saturation for the small display size
4. Optionally add a very small, subtle `hugodaoust.com` in bottom-right (white, low opacity)

## What to AVOID

- Text-heavy banners ("Hire me!" / "Creative Developer" etc.) -- looks like a billboard, not a professional
- Stock photos or abstract gradients -- you have real work, use it
- Busy compositions that become unreadable at banner size
- Bright/white backgrounds that clash with the LinkedIn UI
- Images where the main subject falls in the bottom-left (hidden by profile photo)
